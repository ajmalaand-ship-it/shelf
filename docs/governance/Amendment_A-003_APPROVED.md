1. Insert mandatory **PC — Product Completion & Owner Acceptance** between the P1–P5 technical foundations and P6.
2. Preserve all valid P1–P5 technical work. Technical capability and synthetic QA acceptance do not constitute final real-content product acceptance.
3. Require protected unpublished owner preview, real-catalogue/product review, closure of the accepted Version 1 scope, and Ajmal's explicit **Product Complete / Ready for Release Preparation** approval before P6.
4. Preserve P0 as open/partial where real content, source recovery, owner verification or external launch inputs remain unresolved.
5. Treat Ajmal's original voice recordings as ongoing editorial content. The former fixed minimum of ten recordings is no longer a launch blocker.
6. Preserve the approved P5 sequencing decision: real Google Play and RevenueCat configuration and sandbox acceptance remain deferred to P6, must stay fail-closed until configured, and must pass before final closed testing or production submission.
7. Define P6 as Android release preparation and launch work, not unfinished product development. P7 remains later.

This amendment weakens no architecture, security, privacy, authorship, system-isolation or paid-content-protection boundary.
